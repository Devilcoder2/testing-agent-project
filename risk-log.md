# Sentinel External Risk Log

**Status:** Active  
**Started:** 2026-08-30  
**Reason:** Phase 26 introduces public traffic, personal lead data, Cloudflare Turnstile, Cloudflare Stream, production origins, and legal launch content that sit outside Sentinel's existing controlled local-pilot boundary.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood / impact | Prevention and fallback | Launch gate / owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-001 | Turnstile is missing, misconfigured, unavailable, or validated only in the browser. | Medium / High | Require a server-only secret and Siteverify call for every accepted submission; verify configured action and hostname; reject on validation or provider timeout; use official test keys in automated/local tests. Never bypass validation in production. | Production site/secret keys and successful pass/fail acceptance checks are required. Owner: deployment administrator. |
| R-002 | Public submissions create spam, enumeration, or resource pressure. | Medium / Medium | Exact-origin CORS, no credentials, small JSON body, strict schema, honeypot, infrastructure rate limit, Turnstile, organization/email uniqueness, and the same HTTP 202 response for new and duplicate addresses. | Rate-limit rule and repeated/invalid-origin checks must pass before public access. Owner: deployment administrator. |
| R-003 | Lead PII leaks across Sentinel organizations, logs, audits, exports, or client errors. | Low / High | Bind every lead to one configured owner organization; require active Admin membership in that exact organization; include organization in every query predicate; exclude PII from audit details and safe errors; provide no export or bulk outreach. | Cross-organization API tests, log review, and authorized deletion must pass. Owner: Sentinel maintainer. |
| R-004 | The walkthrough or screenshot exposes credentials, customer data, private evidence, source content, or tokens. | Medium / High | Capture only seeded local fixtures; review every frame and caption; use a dedicated poster; exclude evidence payloads and integration credentials; do not upload until the media review checklist is signed off. | Final video, poster, captions, and screenshot review are required. Owner: product owner. |
| R-005 | Stream is unavailable, blocked, slow, or incurs unplanned delivery cost. | Medium / Medium | Do not load the player before interaction; keep the poster and complete written product story usable; present a bounded fallback when no video ID or playback error exists; monitor delivered minutes through the provider dashboard. | Fallback and lazy-load browser checks must pass; cost owner confirms the provider account. Owner: product owner. |
| R-006 | Marketing copy implies unsupported intelligence, customer proof, availability, security certification, or results. | Medium / High | Source every capability from `srd.md` and the current implementation; prohibit metrics, logos, testimonials, pricing, dates, broad self-healing, certification, or general-availability claims; keep CTA as a reviewed private-pilot waitlist. | Final copy is diff-reviewed against implemented behavior. Owner: product owner and maintainer. |
| R-007 | Privacy or contact content is incomplete for a global public lead form. | High until supplied / High | Keep the site private; ship a visible privacy route and consent sentence; collect only qualification fields; send no automated email or analytics; support confirmed deletion; require approved legal identity, contact, retention wording, and production privacy copy. | Missing legal/contact/privacy content blocks public deployment. Owner: product owner. |
| R-008 | Publishing the landing page is mistaken for production readiness of the authenticated product. | Medium / High | Deploy marketing and product separately; label access as private pilot; keep sign-in external; preserve all unresolved identity, evidence-retention, provider, learning, and shipping gates in their existing documents. | Release report must state that Phase 26 does not close the product shipping gate. Owner: product owner. |
| R-009 | A second application in the repository causes dependency, build, or deployment drift. | Medium / Medium | Give `marketing/` independent scripts, lockfile, tests, and hosting metadata; do not move product sources; run both builds/type checks; document separate environment contracts and deployment order. | Both production builds and clean Git status are required. Owner: maintainer. |
| R-010 | Motion, video dialogs, alternating grids, or custom fonts harm accessibility or page speed. | Medium / Medium | Lazy-load Motion; honor reduced motion; keep logical DOM order; use accessible dialog primitives; self-host subset fonts; lazy-create Stream; test keyboard, captions, 200% zoom, 320px width, Core Web Vitals, and Lighthouse. | WCAG/browser scenarios and agreed performance thresholds must pass. Owner: frontend maintainer. |

## Review cadence

Review this log before the private preview, when final video/legal content arrives, before the public access change, and whenever a provider, domain, form field, data-retention policy, or marketing claim changes. Close a risk only with recorded verification or an approved replacement control; a successful local demo is not sufficient evidence for a public dependency.
